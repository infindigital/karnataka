ರಾಜ್ಯ ಅಧ್ಯಕ್ಷರ ನುಡಿ

ನಮ್ಮ ನಡೆ ಕನ್ನಡದೆಡೆ

ಕನ್ನಡವೇ ನಮ್ಮ ಉಸಿರು, ಕನ್ನಡವೇ ನಮ್ಮ ಶಕ್ತಿ. ನಾಡು-ನುಡಿಯ ರಕ್ಷಣೆ ನಮ್ಮ ಪ್ರಥಮ ಕರ್ತವ್ಯ.

ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ರಕ್ಷಣಾ ವೇದಿಕೆಯು ಕನ್ನಡ ನಾಡು, ನುಡಿ ಮತ್ತು ನೆಲದ ರಕ್ಷಣೆಗಾಗಿ ಸದಾ ಕಾಲ ಶ್ರಮಿಸುತ್ತಿದೆ.

ಕನ್ನಡಿಗರ ಸಬಲೀಕರಣ ಮತ್ತು ಅವರ ಹಕ್ಕುಗಳ ರಕ್ಷಣೆ ನಮ್ಮ ಸಂಘಟನೆಯ ಮುಖ್ಯ ಉದ್ದೇಶಗಳಲ್ಲೊಂದು.

Who We Are

A movement born from the soil of Karnataka.

Founded in 1999 by T. A. Narayana Gowda, Karnataka Rakshana Vedike (KaRaVe) rose to fill the vacuum in the Kannada movement and give ordinary Kannadigas an organised, dignified voice. What began as a handful of volunteers is today one of the largest grassroots organisations in the state.

We are not a political party. We are farmers, students, workers, teachers, auto drivers and entrepreneurs — united by a single belief: that Karnataka's language, resources and people deserve to come first in their own homeland.

Our Cause

Everything we fight for begins with one promise — Kannada first, always.

01

Vision

ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನಿ ಕನ್ನಡ ನಾಡು

A self-respecting Karnataka where the Kannada language thrives, and where every Kannadiga lives with dignity, opportunity and pride.

02

Mission

ನಾಡು ನುಡಿ ಜನ ಸೇವೆ

To organise Kannadigas peacefully and lawfully to protect their language, land, water and right to work in their own state.

03

Values

ಶಿಸ್ತು · ಸೇವೆ · ಸ್ವಾಭಿಮಾನ

Discipline, service and self-respect. We work within the law, without hatred, and always in the spirit of brotherhood.

What We Do

Six fronts, one fight.

From boardrooms to street corners, we turn Kannada pride into concrete action across the state.

01📚

Kannada in Education

Campaigning for Kannada as the medium and a mandatory subject, so no child grows up a stranger to their mother tongue.

02🏢

Jobs for Kannadigas

Pressing companies and government to reserve jobs for locals under the Sarojini Mahishi report and beyond.

03🪧

Kannada Signboards

Ensuring 60%+ Kannada on every commercial nameboard, so our cities look and feel like Karnataka.

04⚖️

Kannada in Administration

Demanding Kannada in courts, offices and banks — governance in the language of the governed.

05🚰

Land, Water & Rights

Standing with farmers on the Cauvery, Mahadayi and every drop that belongs to Karnataka.

06🤝

Community & Relief

Blood drives, disaster relief and support for the poorest — service beyond slogans.

From a spark to a state-wide flame.

    T. A. Narayana Gowda founds Karnataka Rakshana Vedike to give the Kannada movement a disciplined, organised voice.
